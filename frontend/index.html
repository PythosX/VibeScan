How VibeScan works

  1. Add the text

    Paste a bio, or upload a screenshot. Text in screenshots is read inside your browser.

  2. Check what was read

    Edit anything the scanner got wrong, and remove names or contact details before you continue.

  3. Analyse in context

    An AI model reads each phrase alongside the sentences around it, then explains what it may mean.

  4. Explore and ask

    Open any highlighted phrase, compare readings, and copy the questions you want to ask.

Why context matters

A phrase is rarely good or bad on its own. VibeScan reads sentences, not keywords.

“No drama”

A preference for calm conversation, or a way of dismissing feelings. The words around it decide which.

“Alpha”

Confidence and identity for some people, entitlement for others. Never a verdict by itself.

“Sapiosexual”

Attraction to intelligence. A description, not a warning sign.

Cultural vocabulary and unconventional preferences are not red flags. VibeScan is built to say so.

Privacy first

Your profile analysis is temporary. We don’t save uploaded profiles by default.

  • No account, no database. Nothing is stored on our servers after the analysis finishes.
  • Screenshots stay on your device. Text is recognised in your browser. Only the text you approve is sent for analysis.
  • One AI provider, disclosed. Text is processed by Cloudflare Workers AI using Meta’s Llama 3.3 model.
  • Your results live in one tab. The report is kept in this browser tab’s session storage so you can read it. Clear it any time, or close the tab.
  • No analytics on profile text. Profile contents are never logged or sent to analytics.

Frequently asked questions

Does VibeScan tell me if someone is toxic?

No. It interprets language and suggests questions. It can’t know a person’s intentions or character, and it won’t score or diagnose anyone.

Is it accurate?

It’s an AI model, so it can misread tone, slang or humour. Treat results as a starting point for your own judgement and for asking questions.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Text is extracted in your browser. The image never leaves your device; the extracted text is only sent after you choose to analyse it.

Which platforms does it work with?

Any. VibeScan doesn’t connect to dating apps or scrape them. You supply the text yourself.

Is it free?

Yes. It runs on a free AI allowance, which has a daily limit. If the limit is reached you’ll see a clear message and can try again later.

Who is it for?

Adults aged 18 and over. You’ll be asked to confirm your age before first use.
